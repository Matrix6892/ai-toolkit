# What actually happened last season
Application A184 in Sound and application A185 in Moving Image were both for project P62, “Night Windows”. Each had a different track-specific statement; the maker credit and project image were shared.
A184 had two scores, 8 and 6. A reviewer later declared a personal connection. The sheet still displayed 7 until Mira manually removed that review. There is no agreed rule for whether a replacement review is necessary.
A185 was marked “accepted” in a chair's sheet. That meant eligible for review. Jo read it as selected for the public showcase and drafted a congratulatory message. It was caught before sending.
P62's maker corrected the title while reviews were in progress. One reviewer quoted the old title. Nobody can tell whether the statement also changed or just the title.
A chair once added a project to the shortlist despite a lower average, explaining that it completed the range of work. The reason lived in a meeting chat; the spreadsheet showed only YES.
Jo's public page contained a newer maker credit than the jury sheet. That was intentional. Last year's public pages had about 12,000 visits on release morning; there were roughly 900 entries and 80 reviewers.
