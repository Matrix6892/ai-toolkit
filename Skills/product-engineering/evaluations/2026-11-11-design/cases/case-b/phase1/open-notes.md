# Unsettled planning notes
“Accepted” appears in three spreadsheets with different meanings. We have not agreed which labels applicants should see, or who can reverse each kind of decision.
People disagree about edits after submission: lock everything, allow harmless corrections, or let chairs decide. We have not listed what reviewers must be able to reconstruct later.
Review visibility, tie handling, missing reviews and the relationship between scores and jury decisions have not been signed off. Some chairs want their own rules.
Public results are usually announced together. Communications has not yet confirmed whether publication is final, whether tracks can publish separately, or how later corrections should appear.
The workspace need not send email or replace the public website in its first slice. We need to decide what evidence would show that the first slice helps the next call.
