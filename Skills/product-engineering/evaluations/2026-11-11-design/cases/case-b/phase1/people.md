# Notes from people involved
Mira, call coordinator: “Applicants should fix typos themselves. Track chairs decide eligibility. I often help people after the deadline, but I do not want to accidentally change what reviewers saw.”
Noor, track chair: “I invite reviewers and decide which projects reach discussion. Scores help, but a beautiful average can hide one serious concern. The jury makes the shortlist together.”
Eli, reviewer: “Please show me only my assigned work. I should not see other opinions before writing mine. Sometimes I discover I know the maker and must step aside.”
Jo, communications editor: “A public result needs a readable title, maker credit, a short description and an image. I correct these a lot. I need to prepare pages before results go live.”
An applicant: “I applied to two tracks. One says accepted and the other says pending. Am I in? Will editing my project wipe out the first decision?”
The director: “Make it transparent, fair, fast and easy to correct. Ideally everyone has one source of truth. I also want a live dashboard of how the call is doing.”
