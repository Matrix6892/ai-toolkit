# Answers agreed after the planning meeting
A submission belongs to one track; a project groups its submissions and shared descriptive material. “Eligible”, “shortlisted by jury” and “published in the showcase” are separate facts. A shortlist is a recorded jury decision with a reason, not a calculated score threshold.
Track chairs own eligibility and jury decisions. Reviewers own drafts and submit their own reviews; a chair can exclude a review with a reason, never rewrite its contents. Conflict declarations hide that review from aggregates immediately. Chairs choose and record whether a replacement is needed. Reviewers see only their assignments and their own reviews before discussion; chairs can open discussion visibility for their track.
Applicants can change descriptive material. Track statements and other material considered by reviewers must retain the submitted revision and the revision each review considered. Changes affecting eligibility or assessment require chair acknowledgement before the changed revision is considered. The exact list of harmless fields still needs agreement.
Communications owns public wording and maker approval of credits, and cannot alter jury selection. Chairs approve which selected submissions are ready for publication. The first slice may keep invitations and email manual, but must support one track end to end and a safe handoff to communications.
