# New release requirement: announced results stay legible
The director has agreed that the public showcase can no longer be a view of the current working records. Once a release is announced, visitors must keep seeing the exact approved selection and public wording from that release while the team works on changes.
This matters immediately: after the first track publishes, a jury may reopen a selection because a reviewer declared a conflict. Reopening must not silently remove the announced result. Communications may also prepare a corrected credit while an unrelated new selection is under discussion.
A public correction is a separately approved replacement release with a visible explanation and a link to the superseded release. The previous release remains readable. Publish only approved public material; private reviews, conflict details and assessment revisions must never be exposed through either release.
Tracks may announce on different days. A public project page must identify which release supplies each displayed track result; a Sound correction must not accidentally publish Moving Image's draft. Communications authorizes the release after the chair approves its selections and the maker approves public credits. These approvals must identify the content they approved; editing it afterward invalidates the affected approval.
Do not prescribe a deployment topology. Show what this changes in your earlier direction, what still holds, and how the riskiest behavior could be checked before building the full system.
